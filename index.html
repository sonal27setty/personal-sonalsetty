How the estimates work & research

Expected people = RSVPs × turnout rate + walk-ins. Target servings = expected people × servings per person × (1 + buffer rate). Packs = target servings divided by pack size, rounded up. Cost = packs × price per pack. Extra servings = packed servings minus expected consumption; this is a planning cushion, not measured waste.

Compare your actual attendance with expectations and review the leftover share after each event. A repeated high leftover share suggests reviewing portion sizes, pack sizes, or your next order. A low leftover share alone cannot tell you whether everyone got enough food.

The EPA recommends preventing wasted food through planning and tracking how much food is wasted and why. This app applies that approach to campus event orders; it does not measure emissions or prove that food waste was avoided.
